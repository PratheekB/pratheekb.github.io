About

Pratheek is a Senior Software Engineer at NVIDIA, working on GPU memory management. He received his Ph.D. from the Indian Institute of Science (IISc), Bengaluru, in 2026.

His research focuses on GPU memory management, particularly address translation and data placement for large-memory workloads, and spans GPU micro-architecture, compiler techniques, and operating systems. He previously worked on reverse-engineering NVIDIA GPUs, and maintains an active interest in CPU micro-architecture research.
